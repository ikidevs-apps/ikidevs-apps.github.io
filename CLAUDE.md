# CLIMB — Product Description for AI Context

## What CLIMB Is

CLIMB is a mobile study tracker app built around a 
mountain climbing metaphor. The core idea is simple: 
studying = climbing a mountain. Every minute a user 
studies, their character climbs higher up a mountain. 
When they reach the top, they've completed their 
study goal.

The app is designed to make studying feel like a 
journey with a clear destination, visual progress, 
and a sense of adventure — rather than just a timer 
counting down.

---

## The Core Experience

When a user opens the app, they see a beautiful 
illustrated mountain landscape from ground level — 
they are standing far away, looking at the mountain 
they will climb. This is their home screen.

When they tap CLIMB!, a cinematic camera animation 
plays — the view rushes toward the mountain and 
arrives at its slope, as if the user is now there 
in person. This transitions them into their study 
session. The mountain now fills the screen from the 
climber's perspective.

During a study session, a small character icon 
slowly climbs up the mountain in real time as the 
user studies. The higher the character is on the 
mountain, the more the user has studied. When the 
user takes a break, the character stops and sets up 
a small camp with a campfire at that exact position. 
When the user resumes studying, a flag is left at 
the camp spot as a checkpoint, and the character 
continues climbing upward.

When the user finally reaches their study goal, 
their character arrives at the peak of the mountain. 
They receive a notification, a celebration moment, 
and a reward measured in meters climbed.

---

## The Two Climbing Modes

### Standard Mode
The user sets a study goal — for example, "I want 
to study for 3 hours today." The app generates a 
mountain whose peak represents that 3-hour goal. 
The user climbs toward the peak. When they reach 
it, the mountain is complete. They can take breaks 
(camping) and resume as many times as needed within 
the session.

### Climbfinity Mode
There is no peak. The mountain goes on forever. 
The user climbs as long as they want, accumulating 
total meters. Their lifetime meters keep growing 
across all sessions, giving a sense of long-term 
progress and endless ambition. The camping and 
flag mechanics are the same as Standard mode.

---

## Progress and Rewards

Every minute of studying earns the user 1 meter 
of climbing distance. These meters accumulate 
across all sessions and all time. The user's 
total lifetime meters are always displayed on 
the home screen as a key achievement metric — 
a number that only ever grows.

Users earn badges for achievements such as:
- Completing their first mountain
- Studying more than a certain number of hours 
  in one day
- Staying consistent for multiple days in a row
- Accumulating large total meter counts

---

## The Activity Grid

Users can see their last 30 days of activity as 
a color-coded grid — gray for inactive days, 
red tones for low-productivity days, and green 
tones for strong study days. Tapping any day 
shows the details of what the user did that day. 
This works like a fitness app's activity heatmap 
but adapted for studying.

---

## Tasks

Users can create lightweight tasks — things they 
need to study or complete. Tasks have a title, 
optional description, and a deadline. Tasks can 
optionally be linked to a mountain session, so 
the user can see how much study time they've 
dedicated to a specific task. Tasks can also 
exist independently without being linked to 
any mountain.

---

## Profile and Customization

Each user has a profile showing their display name, 
total meters climbed, and their badge collection. 
Users can customize their climber avatar — the 
small character that climbs the mountain — choosing 
from different visual options. The profile also 
shows the full history of past mountains completed, 
with details like how long they studied, how many 
tasks they completed, and where they took breaks.

---

## The Visual Identity

The entire app is built around a warm, premium, 
minimal aesthetic. The mountain illustrations are 
flat-design artwork in warm earthy tones — peach 
skies, warm brown mountain slopes, sage green 
background mountains, white snow caps. The app 
feels like a high-quality iOS experience — smooth 
animations, glassmorphism cards, rounded corners, 
and the Nunito font throughout.

The key visual moment of the app is the transition 
from the home screen (looking at the mountain from 
far away) to the climbing screen (being on the 
mountain slope) — a cinematic camera zoom that 
makes the user feel like they are physically 
traveling to the mountain to begin their climb.

---

## What the App Does NOT Do (in V1)

- No social features or friends (coming in V2)
- No cooperative climbing with friends (V2)
- No Pomodoro mode (V2)
- No leaderboard (V2)
- No competitive elements
- No subscription or payment (V1 is fully free)

---

## The Emotional Goal

CLIMB is designed to make studying feel meaningful, 
visual, and rewarding. The mountain metaphor gives 
every study session a clear beginning, middle, and 
end. The camping mechanic makes breaks feel like 
a natural part of the journey rather than a failure. 
Reaching the peak is genuinely satisfying — not 
just a number going up, but a visual destination 
reached.

The app is for students who want to track their 
study time in a way that feels more alive than a 
plain timer.
